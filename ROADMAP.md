# Roadmap

The authoritative stage plan, gates, project facts, architecture decisions, and release strategy are in [FORA6_MASTER_ROADMAP.md](FORA6_MASTER_ROADMAP.md).

Stages 0–6B3 and 7A–7F are complete for their authorized bounded/review scopes. Stage 6B3's in-place migration to factory Bluetooth MAC identity was validated by the user in Home Assistant on one GD82. Population-wide fixed-address behavior, reset/update behavior, and uniqueness remain unproven. General traversal, deduplication, production synchronization, and polling remain absent. **Next proposed gate:** separately authorize Stage 7G implementation/review of a manual current-state refresh for the tested counts only, requiring exactly one eligible General uric-acid primary. Production historical sync and automatic triggers remain unauthorized.
