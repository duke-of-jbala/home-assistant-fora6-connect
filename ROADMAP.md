The authoritative stage plan, gates, project facts, architecture decisions, and release strategy are in [FORA6_MASTER_ROADMAP.md](FORA6_MASTER_ROADMAP.md).

Stage 0 and **Stage 1 — FORA 6 Connect BLE discovery** are complete. Fresh Auto-mode advertisement and Bluetooth address identity remain Stage 6 work; exact selected scanner/proxy remains Stage 9 work. **Stage 2 is in progress:** Stages 2A–2G are complete for their authorized scope. Stage 2F physically retrieved valid User1/index-zero record frames with the GD82 ON, without `0x33`. After private app sources became available, Stage 2G added offline, evidence-backed record-part decoding and synthetic tests; the live probe still exposes no health data. **Exact next gate:** review Stage 2G and separately authorize Stage 3 wider fixture/record-model work or a focused evidence follow-up. No new physical BLE test, production sync, polling, pairing, or RACP is authorized.
