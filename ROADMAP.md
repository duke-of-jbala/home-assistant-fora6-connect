# Roadmap

The authoritative stage plan, gates, project facts, architecture decisions, and release strategy are in [FORA6_MASTER_ROADMAP.md](FORA6_MASTER_ROADMAP.md).

Stages 0, 1, 3, **Stage 4 implementation**, and Stages 2A–2G are complete for their authorized scopes. Unresolved protocol semantics remain open/deferred. Stage 4 added a mock-tested reusable Bluetooth session and refactored the bounded development probes without new commands or health-data exposure. **Exact next gate:** review Stage 4 and separately authorize a controlled user-run identity regression, optionally followed by the already-proven one-slot record probe. Stage 5 measurement/entity work requires separate authorization.
