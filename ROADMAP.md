# Roadmap

The authoritative stage plan, gates, project facts, architecture decisions, and release strategy are in [FORA6_MASTER_ROADMAP.md](FORA6_MASTER_ROADMAP.md).

Stage 0 and **Stage 1 — FORA 6 Connect BLE discovery** are complete. Stage 1A established the real local name, connectable advertisement, sanitized advertisement fields, and Home Assistant visibility in controlled Active mode. Stage 1B validated Home Assistant connection, five-service GATT inventory, and clean disconnect. Stage 1C completed bounded passive observation: standard `2A18`/`2A34` subscriptions failed, custom `1524` subscribed, and no notification arrived during navigation of the only existing uric-acid result. Fresh Auto-mode advertisement and Bluetooth address identity behavior are deferred to Stage 6; the selected scanner/proxy to Stage 9; manufacturer-data meaning remains unknown and may matter to Stage 6 identification. **Stage 2 is in progress for public evidence acquisition.** Manufacturer documentation confirms the custom GATT metadata and iFORA HM model association, but no exact GD82 command bytes were verified. **Exact next gate: explicit authorization of Stage 2B static acquisition and inspection of iFORA HM.** No live protocol operation or pairing is authorized.
