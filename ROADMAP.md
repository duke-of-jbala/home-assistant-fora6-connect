# Roadmap

The authoritative stage plan, gates, project facts, architecture decisions, and release strategy are in [FORA6_MASTER_ROADMAP.md](FORA6_MASTER_ROADMAP.md).

Stage 0 is complete. **Stage 1 — FORA 6 Connect BLE discovery** is in progress. Home Assistant detected `FORA 6 CONNECT` through the Lounge ESPHome proxy in Active mode after the scan-timing override was removed and firmware was reflashed. A fresh Auto-mode advertisement and Home Assistant-side GATT remain unconfirmed. The next gate is a development-only Stage 1B Home Assistant probe with an on-demand active scan in Auto mode and no application writes. Stage 2 is not authorized.
