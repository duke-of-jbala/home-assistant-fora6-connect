# Home Assistant — FORA 6 Connect: master roadmap

This is the authoritative project roadmap. `ROADMAP.md` points here. Stage gates require explicit user authorization; completing one stage does not start the next. **Stages 0, 1, 2A–2G, 3, 4, 5, 5A, 6A–6B3, and 7A–7F are complete for their authorized bounded/review scopes. Stage 7F identifies only a conditional manual current-state refresh design; broader ordering and dedup remain unresolved.** Stage 4 includes successful user-run physical transport regression. Stage 5A establishes the app's mg/dL base unit for valid uric-acid raw/10 values. The user-run Stage 6A1b comparison found the private `0x2A25` value stable on an immediate repeat and after one meter OFF/ON cycle, but Stage 6B1 later established its content is a generic placeholder. Stage 6B2 found readable, structurally plausible but unusable `0x2A23`. The user validated Stage 6B3 migration on one GD82: the same entry/device/entity remained, Bluetooth connection and model metadata remained, bogus serial was absent, and rediscovery stayed suppressed. The factory BT MAC matches HA's address for this meter; population-wide stability and uniqueness remain unproven. Production record synchronization remains absent and unauthorized.

## Evidence register

### Confirmed project and documentary facts

- The target product is FORA 6 Connect, model/variant GD82. The [ForaCare FAQ Rev 5.5, Q36–Q37](https://www.foracare.ch/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/3.1-BGM-FAQ_Rev5.5_230313.pdf) has now been independently reviewed: it documents service `00001523-1212-efde-1523-785feabcd123`, characteristic `00001524-1212-efde-1523-785feabcd123` with Write/Notify, and associates GD82 with iFORA HM. It does not provide command bytes. See `docs/STAGE2_PROTOCOL_ACQUISITION.md`.
- The integration records these UUIDs and offers manually invoked Stage 1B GATT inventory and Stage 1C notification-metadata actions. Stage 2D adds offline frame/project/scaling helpers. Stage 2E's separate development-only Home Assistant action sent exactly two captured custom `1524` requests on the real GD82 with the meter ON and confirmed project `0x4183`. The Stage 2F development-only one-slot probe subsequently retrieved valid User1/index-zero `0x2B`/`0x25`/`0x26` response frames on the real meter, without decoding a record. Stage 6B adds a guarded production Bluetooth candidate matcher and an initially unavailable uric-acid entity. There is still no production record synchronization, polling, or decoded real measurement exposure. Stage 2G provides an offline HA-independent record parser only; Stage 3 adds an offline combined model and synthetic fixtures. Earlier builds stopped at discovery; the later direct-connect Stage 1B probe succeeded through Home Assistant without application I/O.
- The UUID pair alone is insufficient proof that an observed BLE device is a FORA 6 Connect; similar UUIDs may be reused.

## Frozen architecture decisions

The accepted decisions are detailed in `docs/DECISIONS.md`:

1. **ADR-001:** Home Assistant performs FORA-specific communication and decoding.
2. **ADR-002:** ESPHome Bluetooth Proxy is transport only and remains generic.
3. **ADR-003:** The protocol parser is independent of Home Assistant.
4. **ADR-004:** Documented or captured evidence with provenance precedes protocol implementation; tests use sanitized evidence-derived fixtures.
5. **ADR-005:** One physical meter is represented by one Home Assistant device, with later entities attached to it.
6. **ADR-006:** Original meter measurement time is preserved separately from synchronization/ingestion time.
7. **ADR-007:** Private health data and identifiable raw BLE captures are excluded from the public repository.

Use supported Home Assistant Bluetooth APIs; do not hard-code a direct local interface. Future connections must work through eligible proxies and follow then-current retry-safe HA/Bleak guidance. A UUID match alone must never claim device identity. Where the protocol provides test/control-solution status, those readings must not silently become ordinary health readings.

## Privacy and evidence rule

Do not commit identifiable health measurements, secrets, meter identifiers, MAC addresses from private captures, or raw private BLE captures. Keep raw material outside Git. Sanitize any public fixture, record its provenance and transformations, and verify that sanitization did not invalidate the tested behavior. Record observed facts separately from interpretations and hypotheses.
